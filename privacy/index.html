Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Welcome to Wallverse. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Wallverse mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Wallverse, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture, if provided
  • Information provided through Google Sign-In, such as your email address, display name, and profile picture
  • Wallpapers uploaded by users
  • Comments, likes, follows, collections, and other community interactions
  • Technical data such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display user profiles and uploaded wallpapers
  • Enable likes, comments, follows, collections, and other community features
  • Moderate content and enforce our community guidelines
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and unauthorized activity
  • Display advertisements

3. User-Generated Content

Wallverse allows users to upload and share wallpapers and interact with other users. Content you upload, your username, profile picture, comments, and public activity may be visible to other users.

Please do not upload or share personal, sensitive, or confidential information through public content.

4. Third-Party Services

Wallverse uses third-party services to operate and improve the app, including:

  • Supabase, for authentication, database, and file storage
  • Google Sign-In, for account authentication
  • Google AdMob, for advertising

These third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

Wallverse uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may collect and use certain information to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here: Google Privacy Policy .

6. Data Storage and Security

We store user data using trusted third-party infrastructure providers. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers necessary to operate Wallverse
  • Advertising partners
  • Legal authorities if required by law
  • Security or fraud prevention services when necessary to protect the app and its users

8. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us at: wallverse.app@outlook.com.

When your account is deleted, your personal information will be deleted or anonymized, unless we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

9. Children’s Privacy

Wallverse is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: wallverse.app@outlook.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: wallverse.app@outlook.com.
