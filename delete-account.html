Delete Your Wallverse Account

Users may request deletion of their Wallverse account and associated personal data by contacting:

wallverse.app@outlook.com

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your Wallverse account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Account".
  3. Include your Wallverse username if available.

What Happens Next

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days.

Once processed:

  • Your account will be deleted.
  • Your personal information will be removed or anonymized.
  • Your uploaded content may be removed from the platform.

Certain information may be retained when required by law or for security and fraud-prevention purposes.
